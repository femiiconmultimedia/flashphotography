Our Story

A decade of chasing good light

Flash Photography started in 2014 in a one-room studio in Yaba, with a secondhand DSLR, a bedsheet backdrop, and a stubborn belief that the best photographs are the ones people forget were being taken.

Ten years and over 400 shoots later, we're still a small team. We turned down the "big agency" route on purpose. Every wedding, every portrait, every print that leaves our studio passes through the same pairs of hands — and that's exactly how we want it.

Today we shoot across West Africa, run monthly training workshops, and press fine-art prints in our own darkroom-grade studio.
