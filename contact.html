FAQ

Questions we get a lot

If your question isn't covered here, message us on WhatsApp — we're quick to respond.

How do I book a shoot?
Send us a message via the form or WhatsApp with your preferred date, the type of shoot, and your location. We'll reply with availability and a quote within 24 hours. A 40% deposit confirms the booking.
How long until I receive my photos?
Weddings and events are delivered within 3 weeks. Portraits, product shoots and brand sessions within 10 days. Rush delivery is available for an extra fee.
Do you travel outside Lagos?
Yes — we shoot all over Nigeria and beyond. Travel within Lagos is included. Outside Lagos, we add a travel fee covering transport and accommodation, which we'll quote upfront.
Can I order prints without doing a shoot with you?
Absolutely. Send us the images you'd like printed (via email or WhatsApp) and tell us the size and finish you want. We'll quote, then print once you approve. Turnaround is 3–5 working days.
What do I need for the training courses?
It depends on the course. For computer training and graphics design, you'll need your own laptop. For photography, a camera (any model). For computer engineering, a basic toolkit. Full details are sent once you enroll.
Do you offer payment plans?
Yes. Training courses can be split into two or three installments. For shoots and print orders above ₦300,000, we also offer flexible payment terms. Just ask.
